# Claimant Slice 2 Decision Register

Last updated: 2026-07-31 (Asia/Dubai)

Status: Product-owner scope approved; security, privacy, legal, operations, and independent approvals remain outstanding.

## Scope And Stop Gate

This register recommends policy and architecture defaults for Claimant Slice 2. It does not authorize claimant authentication, invitations, persistence, migrations, RLS or Storage policies, API routes, evidence upload, notifications, processors, claim submission, review transitions, release packages, or decryption UI.

Claimant capabilities remain hard-disabled. Slice 3 cannot begin until every item marked as a Slice 3 blocker has a named approver, dated decision, linked evidence, and no unresolved critical threat.

## Recommended Product Boundary

- Ship registered-recipient preparation before V2 offline-code initiation.
- Limit the first invitation-only claimant pilot to death claims. Defer incapacity, disappearance, lost-access-but-living-owner, and other exceptional routes until separately advised and approved.
- Treat registration, MFA, recipient status, route possession, identity, relationship, evidence, and release authority as separate controls.
- Never release because the owner did not respond. Failed notice, ambiguity, conflict, dispute, processor failure, or incomplete authority moves the case to `on_hold` or `manual_review`.
- Keep claimant cryptography in the native app. The claimant web origin may later handle account, policy, case, and evidence workflow but never private-key custody or released-vault decryption.
- Require hardware-backed, non-exportable P-256 claimant keys and user presence for each private-key operation.
- Require at least two independently enrolled claimant device keys, with an owner-finalized grant for each, before a live pilot. Do not provide server-recoverable claimant-key escrow.
